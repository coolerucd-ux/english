PointWord — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

PointWord ("the app", "we") is a point-to-read English learning app. You point your finger at an English word in the real world; the app recognizes it and shows a definition. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. We keep this deliberately short because the app collects very little.

Data we send to a third-party AI service

To generate a definition and a short scene caption, the app sends the following to Alibaba Cloud DashScope (Model Studio), an AI service, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection:

  • The word and its surrounding sentence — sent as text to obtain the dictionary definition.
  • The photo captured when you save a word — sent to a vision model to produce a one-line description of the scene (e.g. "a café menu"), used for the reunion banner and the footprints feature.

This data is sent solely to produce the result you asked for. It is not linked to your identity, not used to track you, and we do not attach any account, device identifier, or advertising identifier to it. We do not operate our own server; the app talks to DashScope directly. DashScope's handling of request data is governed by Alibaba Cloud's own terms.

Data that stays on your device

  • Saved words, photos and metadata are stored locally on your device (via Apple's SwiftData). They are not uploaded to us.
  • Location: with your permission, the app uses your location only to look up the name of the city where you saved a word, so it can show your learning "footprints". The city name is stored on your device together with the word. Your precise coordinates are not sent to us or to the AI service.
  • Settings (chosen language, onboarding state) are stored on-device via UserDefaults.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No account, sign-up, name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising or cross-app/website tracking.
  • No analytics SDKs.

Deleting your data

All saved words and photos live on your device. Deleting the app removes them permanently. There is no server-side copy for us to delete.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

PointWord — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 1 日

PointWord（“本应用”）是一款“手指指读”英语学习应用：你用手指指向现实中的英文单词，应用识别后展示释义。本政策说明应用处理哪些数据、数据去向。应用采集的数据极少。

发送给第三方 AI 服务的数据

为生成释义和一句话场景描述，应用会通过加密（HTTPS）连接，将以下内容发送给 阿里云 DashScope（百炼 Model Studio）这一 AI 服务：

  • 单词及其所在句子——以文本形式发送，用于获取词典释义。
  • 收藏单词时拍摄的画面——发送给视觉模型，用于生成一句话场景描述（如“咖啡馆的菜单”），供“重逢横幅”和“足迹”功能使用。

这些数据仅用于生成你请求的结果，不关联你的身份、不用于追踪，不附带任何账号、设备标识或广告标识。我们不运营自有服务器，应用直接与 DashScope 通信；DashScope 对请求数据的处理适用阿里云自身条款。

仅保存在你设备上的数据

  • 收藏的单词、照片及元数据通过 Apple 的 SwiftData 存储在本机，不会上传给我们。
  • 位置：在你授权后，应用仅用位置反查你收藏单词时所在的城市名称，用于展示学习“足迹”。城市名与单词一起存在本机；你的精确坐标不会发送给我们或 AI 服务。
  • 设置（所选语言、引导状态）通过 UserDefaults 存储在本机。

我们不采集的数据

  • 不需要账号、注册，不采集姓名、邮箱或手机号。
  • 无广告，无跨应用/网站追踪。
  • 无第三方统计 SDK。

删除你的数据

所有收藏单词与照片都在你的设备上，卸载应用即彻底删除；我们没有服务端副本。
