How to use PointWord

  • Point the camera at printed English text (a book, sign, screen, or menu).
  • Rest your fingertip just under a word and hold steady for a moment.
  • A card appears with the pronunciation, meaning, and how the word is used in that sentence.
  • Tap the heart to save a word. Saved words gather in your collection and on your "footprints" map by place and month.

Frequently asked

The camera shows but nothing is recognized. Make sure the text is printed English (not handwriting), well lit, and roughly in focus, and that your fingertip is close under the target word.

Definitions won't load. The app needs an internet connection to look up words. Check your network and try again.

Do you upload my photos? Only the single frame captured when you save a word is sent to an AI service to describe the scene. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data? Everything is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes all saved words and photos permanently.

如何使用

  • 将摄像头对准印刷体英文（书本、标识、屏幕或菜单）。
  • 把指尖停在某个单词下方，稳定停留片刻。
  • 会弹出卡片，显示读音、释义，以及该词在这句话里的用法。
  • 点爱心即可收藏；收藏的单词会汇入“收藏”，并按地点与月份出现在你的“足迹”里。

常见问题

相机开着但识别不出？ 请确保是印刷体英文（非手写）、光线充足、大致对焦，且指尖贴近目标单词下方。

释义加载不出来？ 查词需要联网，请检查网络后重试。

会上传我的照片吗？ 仅在你收藏单词时，把那一帧画面发送给 AI 服务用于描述场景，详见隐私政策。

如何删除我的数据？ 所有数据都存在你的设备上，卸载应用即彻底删除全部收藏单词与照片。
