**Promotional Text**（≤170）
```
Reading English in the real world? Just point your finger at any word to see what it means—then save it and revisit where you found it.
```

**Description**（≤4000）
```
PointWord turns the English around you into your dictionary. See a word you don't know—on a book, a sign, a menu, a screen—just point your finger at it. PointWord recognizes the word and instantly shows you the pronunciation, meaning, and how it's used in that exact sentence.

No typing. No copy-paste. No switching apps. Point, and learn.

• POINT TO READ
Rest your fingertip under any printed English word and hold for a moment. A clean card appears with the IPA pronunciation, part of speech, dictionary meanings, and a plain-language note on what the word means in this context.

• SAVE WHAT YOU MEET
Tap the heart to keep a word. Every saved word remembers the moment you found it—the photo, the place, the time.

• YOUR WORD FOOTPRINTS
Saved words gather into a timeline by place and month, like a travel diary of your English learning. See the words you met at the café, on the subway, in that book.

• WORD REUNIONS
Run into a word you've saved before? PointWord greets you—"you've met this one 3 times"—and lets you revisit every place you encountered it, photo by photo.

• LISTEN
Tap to hear any word pronounced.

• YOUR LANGUAGE
Explanations are shown in your native language—Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Korean, Japanese, French, Spanish, Portuguese, or Italian.

PointWord is built for learners who read English in the wild: students, travelers, anyone who wants to turn everyday reading into real vocabulary. No account, no sign-up—open the app and start pointing.
```

PointWord 把你身边的英语变成你的词典。看到不认识的单词——书上、招牌、菜单、屏幕上——用手指指一下就行。PointWord 会识别它，立刻显示读音、释义，以及它在这句话里的具体用法。

• 手指指读
把指尖停在任意印刷体英文单词下方，稍作停留，就会弹出一张清爽的卡片：音标、词性、词典释义，以及它在当前语境里的通俗解释。

PointWord 为在真实世界里阅读英语的学习者而做：学生、旅行者，以及每一个想把日常阅读变成真实词汇量的人。无需账号、无需注册——打开应用，指一下就开始。

## 审核备注（App 审核信息 → 备注）

```
This app's core feature requires pointing the phone's camera at PRINTED ENGLISH TEXT (a book, sign, menu, or screen) and resting a fingertip just under a word. Holding steady for a moment triggers recognition and shows a definition card.

To test: please aim the camera at any printed English paragraph and hold your fingertip under a word. A card with pronunciation and meaning will appear. An internet connection is required for word lookup.

No account or login is needed.
```
